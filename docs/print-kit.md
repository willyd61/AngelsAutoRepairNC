# Print kit

Vectors only. Business cards are 3.5 × 2 inch trim inside a 3.75 × 2.25 inch
bleed (1125 × 675 px at 300 dpi). The quarter flyer is 4.25 × 5.5. The
postcard file includes bleed for a 4 × 6.

Every QR opens <https://angels-auto.cbgoc-cloud.top/>.

Backs are navy, with the QR, the URL, and a notes line. None of them print a
dollar amount. A price without an inspection is how this kind of card gets
someone angry.

## Ten ways to make the card stand out

1. Work-order back. A notes line and a QR. No price. The photo is the
   conversation.
2. Bridge card. Stay on the island. Snow's Cut is for the beach, not a parts
   run.
3. Saturday card. The closing hour is the headline, so nobody treats 4:30 like
   a reservation they already hold.
4. Neighbor card. The check-engine light that has been on since July. Hand it
   to the person who has been waiting the light out.
5. Salt-air card. Rotors that took a beach week, then got measured instead of
   guessed.
6. Two-bay card. The shop confirms the drop-off. It does not sell a slot it
   does not have.
7. Visitor card. Checkout tomorrow is a fact. Say what two bays can actually
   see before someone misses a ferry or a rental return.
8. Orange-wrench card. Navy field, one tool, the phone set huge. This one
   lives in a toolbox and a counter jar.
9. Pocket estimate. The number is missing on purpose. The QR opens the
   request, not a coupon.
10. Round coaster. Same QR as the card, so the waiting table and the handshake
    tell one story.

Ideas 1 through 4, plus the flyer, postcard, and coaster, are files. Ideas 5
through 9 are the next prints. Build them in `branding/build_brand.py` so they
keep this plate.
